## Important Findings

- The Pixel 7a videos saved as about 30 fps, not 60 fps. For reporting, use the file metadata value: 1920x1080, H.264, about 30 fps.
- The active-minus-idle USB meter change was very small:
  - T1 delta: 0.007 W.
  - T2 delta: 0.001 W.
- Using a 10 s transmitter window and 748 transmitted bits:
  - T1 active-minus-idle energy per transmitted bit: 9.36e-5 J/bit.
  - T2 active-minus-idle energy per transmitted bit: 1.34e-5 J/bit.
- These deltas are close to the USB meter resolution, so they should be reported as near-resolution incremental receiver-power measurements rather than a strong active-power separation.
- BER is not scored yet for the Pixel 7a videos. Pixel decoding needs a separate video/rolling-shutter pipeline or at least a visibility check; the EVK4 RAW decoder should not be reused for these videos.
